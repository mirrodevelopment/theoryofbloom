How to Brew
  1. Boil fresh water and let it cool to 85–90°C (off the boil for ~60 seconds).
  2. Measure 1 heaped teaspoon (2–3g) of the blend into a strainer or infuser.
  3. Pour 200ml of hot water over the blend.
  4. Steep for 5–7 minutes — longer for a more therapeutic cup.
  5. Remove the infuser, sweeten with honey if desired, and sip slowly.
Best Time
Morning on an empty stomach or 30 minutes before bed.
Temperature
Never use boiling water — 85–90°C protects delicate botanicals.
Consistency
Drink daily for 3–4 weeks to experience the full therapeutic benefits.
Storage
Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Reseal after each use.
How do I brew this blend?

Add 1 tsp (2–3g) to 200ml of hot water (85–90°C). Steep for 5–7 minutes, strain, and sip slowly.

How many servings per pack?

Each 50g pack contains approximately 25 servings. A 100g pack contains ~50 servings — ideal for a 2-month ritual.

Are there any side effects?

Our blends are made from 100% natural ingredients. If pregnant, nursing, or on medication, consult your doctor before use.

Can I drink this every day?

Yes! Our blends are designed for daily use. Most customers notice visible benefits within 2–4 weeks of consistency.

Is this suitable for vegans?

Absolutely. All Theory of Bloom blends are 100% plant-based, vegan, and cruelty-free.
